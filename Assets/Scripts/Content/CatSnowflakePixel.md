# 눈꽃 고양이 시안

순백색 털, 하늘색 눈, 귀와 꼬리의 얼음 결정. 4열 3행 대기/걷기/얼음 마법 공격. Built-in image_gen 제작. Resources/CatSnowflakePixel.png에 저장. 유니크 그림자 고양이(cat_shadow)를 눈꽃 고양이로 변경하여 전투와 카드에 연결했습니다. 기존 레벨/조각/능력치/가격 유지. 명중 시 적 이동 속도를 2초간 40% 낮춥니다. 재명중은 시간을 갱신하며 중첩하지 않습니다. 부활 시 둔화를 초기화합니다.

## 프롬프트

Create a transparent pixel-art game spritesheet of a cute SNOWFLAKE CAT: pure snow-white fur everywhere, very light icy blue shadows only, bright sky-blue eyes, pink inner ears and nose, tiny translucent ice crystal ornaments at ear tips and tail tip. Bipedal upright kitten, big round head small body, no clothing weapons or hat. White fur must stay white not gray. Crisp chunky retro pixel art, cool blue-gray outlines, limited palette, no painterly gradients. EXACT 4 columns by 3 rows uniform square cells, landscape 4:3 canvas, 12 frames of same cat with consistent size and foot baseline. Faces slightly right three-quarter front. Generous 18% padding, all ears tails and magic inside their own cells. Top row four IDLE frames: neutral with tiny snowflake floating above paw, gentle breathing and small cold puff, blink, neutral. Middle row four biped WALK frames alternating feet, carrying no object. Bottom row four ICE MAGIC ATTACK frames: gathers tiny snowflakes between paws, raises front paw creating small ice crystal, extends paw releasing a short pointed blue-white ice shard with compact frosty sparkles, returns to idle. Spell visually distinct from purple magic and orange fire. Exactly one cat per cell, no overlapping frames. Genuine transparent background, no ground shadows, text labels borders or grid. Cute fantasy cat RPG asset.

# 고양이 전용 애니메이션 시트

제작: 내장 image_gen 도구. 투명 PNG, 대기·걷기·공격·피격 각 4프레임.
전투와 카드 이미지는 CatAnimationFrames의 공유 캐시를 사용합니다.
발 위치와 프레임 경계는 실제 생성 이미지 기준으로 보정했습니다.
던전 타워 디펜스 고양이는 피격 판정이 없으므로 피격 모션은 메인 전투와 레이드에서 재생됩니다.

## CatStarterActionPixel

저장 경로: Resources/CatStarterActionPixel.png

최종 생성 프롬프트:

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: production pixel art sprite sheet for cute Korean cat RPG. Create ONE square transparent PNG, exactly 4 columns x 4 rows of equal cells, sixteen full-body sprites of SAME biped cat. No text, labels, borders, floor, shadow or background. Crisp chunky pixel clusters, dark brown outline, adorable large head, small body, three-quarter side view facing RIGHT. Each cell has identical scale and stable torso center x=50%, feet ground line y=88% from top; keep entire pose and accessories within cell with at least 6% margin. Four frames per row. Top row: subtle idle/breath/blink cycle. Second row: four distinct walking poses with alternating feet, torso stays centered. Third row: anticipation, attack extension, impact, recovery (clearly different poses). Bottom row: hit flinch, recoil with squeezed eyes, regain balance, recover. Maintain matching anatomy and costume in ALL frames. Real alpha transparency. Orange tabby cat, cream muzzle chest and paws, no costume, classic basic cat. Third row bare front-paw punch and claw swipe toward right, first draws paw back then extends it, then small cream slash, then returns paw. Bottom row surprised ears back, eyes squeezed, arms guarding chest.

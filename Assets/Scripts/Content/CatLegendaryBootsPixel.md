## 프롬프트

Create a transparent pixel art animation spritesheet for a legendary swashbuckling cat inspired by Puss in Boots. Cute biped orange tabby swordsman, confident green eyes, broad dark brown cavalier hat with red feather, short burgundy cape, leather belt with golden buckle, tall dark brown boots, slim silver rapier with golden cup guard. Chunky readable retro pixel art matching a cute cat RPG, dark pixel outlines and restrained palette, no smooth gradients. Exact 4 columns x 3 rows equal square cells, landscape 4:3. Same character size, clothing, weapon and fixed foot baseline across all 12 frames. Generous 18 percent padding in every cell, weapon and feather MUST remain within own cell. Three-quarter front facing right. Top row four idle frames: ready stance, breathing, blink, ready stance. Middle row four upright biped walk frames alternating boots and arms while holding rapier. Bottom row four sword attack frames: windup sword drawn back, short forward thrust, follow-through with tiny golden crescent spark, recovery. Entire character and rapier inside each cell, compact rapier, no overlapping cells. Genuine transparent background. No text, grid, borders, floor or shadows. Exactly twelve frames. One playable character, no extra cats.
